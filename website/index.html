How it works

Three steps, then you’re masked

1

Download

A 452 KB app for macOS. The render engine streams in once, on first launch.

2

Pick a mask

Eye lasers, hand portals, fire, gravity, filters — masks that react to your face, hands, or the camera.

3

Select the camera

Choose MEETAMASK Camera in Zoom or Meet, and you’re wearing the mask on the call.

Why it’s different

Built to stay on, stay smooth

⌘H

Renders off-screen

The mask renders in a separate engine, off-screen — it isn’t tied to the app window being in front.

Works as a camera

Any app that accepts a virtual camera — Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, OBS.

Face & hands

MediaPipe tracking — masks follow your expression and gestures.

452 KB to start

Tiny download; the GPU engine streams in once, designed for a clean 1280×720 at 30 fps.

47 masks, ready to wear

A library of face, hand, camera and filter effects — all built into the app.
